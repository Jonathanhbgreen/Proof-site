Why we are building Proof

Proof started with a simple problem: most focus and energy drinks rely on caffeine, which can leave you jittery, crashing, or unable to sleep. Students with late classes, professionals with long work sessions, and people who already had their coffee had no clean option for extra focus without stacking more caffeine. Proof is our answer: a non-caffeinated focus and productivity drink in a 12 oz slim can.

What is inside

Alpha-GPC, L-Tyrosine, Rhodiola Rosea extract, L-Theanine, Magnesium L-Threonate, and B vitamins, with every dose disclosed on the label.

Clean by design

Sweetened with stevia and monk fruit. No artificial sweeteners, artificial colors or flavors, or sodium or potassium benzoate.

Three flavors

White Peach, Raspberry Lime, and Pineapple Coconut, each designed to taste great without hiding what is inside.

Where we are today

Proof is an early-stage startup founded in Dallas, Texas by Jonathan Green and Bond Blackman, who are both passionate about health, wellness and fitness. We are currently pre-launch: the formula and branding are designed, and we are working toward a small pilot batch with a campus-first approach starting at SMU.

  • Pilot batch with local students for feedback
  • Third-party testing and fully disclosed labels
  • A broader launch once the pilot proves the product
